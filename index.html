Subac — track Quran recitation sessionsSubac — la soco session-yada Quraanka

Subac is a mobile app for Quran teachers and study circles. Create rooms, add members, run recitation sessions with a timer, record mistakes by category, and see each member's result and progress over time.

Subac waa app mobile ah oo loogu talagalay macallimiinta iyo goobaha barashada Quraanka. Samee qolal, ku dar xubnaha, wadi session leh saacad, diiwaangeli khaladaadka nooc nooc, oo arag natiijada iyo horumarka xubin kasta.

What you can doWaxaad sameyn kartaa

  • Rooms, members and recitation sessions (solo, group and competition).
  • Scoring by mistake categories, with results, rankings and statistics.
  • Optional audio recording of a session, including in the background.
  • Recycle bin, backup and restore (JSON/ZIP) and export to CSV/Excel.
  • Works offline. An optional account syncs your data between your devices.
  • Available in Somali, English and Arabic.
  • Qolal, xubno iyo session-yo akhris ah (hal qof, koox iyo tartan).
  • Dhibcaha oo lagu qiimeeyo noocyada khaladaadka, leh natiijooyin, kaalin (rank) iyo tirakoob.
  • Duubista codka session-ka (ikhtiyaari), xitaa asalka (background).
  • Recycle bin, backup iyo soo celin (JSON/ZIP) iyo soo saarid CSV/Excel.
  • Wuxuu shaqeeyaa offline. Akoon ikhtiyaari ah ayaa xogta ku waafajiya qalabyadaada.
  • Waxaa ku yaal Soomaali, Ingiriis iyo Carabi.

Your dataXogtaada

Your data is stored on your phone first. If you choose to create an account or sign in with Google, it is synced securely to your own private cloud space. We do not show ads and we do not sell your data. Read the details in our Privacy Policy.

Xogtaadu marka hore waxay ku kaydsan tahay telefoonkaaga. Haddii aad doorato inaad akoon samaysato ama Google ku gasho, waxaa si ammaan ah loogu waafajiyaa goobtaada gaarka ah ee cloud-ka. Xayaysiis ma muujino, xogtaadana ma iibino. Faahfaahinta ka akhri Xeerka Gaarka ah (Privacy Policy).

Sign in with Google is used only to identify you (your email and basic profile).

Gelitaanka Google waxaa loo isticmaalaa oo kaliya inaad is-garato (email-kaaga iyo xogta aasaasiga ah ee profile-ka).
